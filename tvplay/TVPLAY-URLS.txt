TVPlay - Quick Reference URLs
=============================================

Web App (PWA - installable, TV-compatible)
  https://appdeveloper365.github.io/tvplay/

IPTV Playlists (paste into TiviMate / VLC / IPTV Smarters / Kodi)
  Sports (worldwide, free FTA + FAST:  Soccer + 5 regional groups)
    https://appdeveloper365.github.io/tvplay/assets/sports-world.m3u
  News (worldwide, free news channels: Global + 5 regional groups)
    https://appdeveloper365.github.io/tvplay/assets/news-world.m3u

Source Repository
  https://github.com/Appdeveloper365/TvPlay

Offline APK build guide
  see apk/BUILD-APK.md in the repo (Capacitor wrapper)

Local testing (this Mac)
  App:      http://localhost:8210
  Network:  http://<mac-ip>:8210   (get IP with: ipconfig getifaddr en0)
  Start:    cd /Users/bt/Downloads/TV && python3 -m http.server 8210 --bind 0.0.0.0

Maintenance
  - Reload the app once after each deploy (service worker cache bump).
  - Settings -> "Scan & Remove Dead Streams" prunes dead channels.
  - Playlists you add in Settings load unfiltered (your sources, your call).
